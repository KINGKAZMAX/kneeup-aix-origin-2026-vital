# 22-英文讲稿与答辩弹药库（2026-09-05）

> 用途：①2.5分钟电梯版（评委停留时）②巡展1分钟版（评委刚站定时）③评委QA Top12英文口头回答。
> 原则：简单词、短句、可背说。事实源：docs/10-16；合规红线：不说 diagnose / treat(tment) / prescribe(pscription) / cure / heal（否定语境的固定免责句除外）。

## 一、2.5分钟电梯版（约350词，2:20-2:40）

> Hi, we are Team Free Will. Meet Zhiyuan. He is 62. After his knee surgery, he said: "I don't want to be just a patient. I want to hike again — smarter."
>
> Here is his reality. In Hong Kong public hospitals, the wait for knee replacement surgery can be **72 months**. Six years. Ten years ago it was 33. And when patients go home, nobody watches. Studies show up to **50%** of people drop off their home exercises. Adherence scores fall from 7.8 to 3.5 in about 18 months. Why? They forget. They are alone. No one corrects their moves.
>
> So we built **AIR-FLOW Knee+** — software that brings rehab training out of the hospital, back into the living room. The patient checks in daily and trains with an AI companion. Live signals — muscle fatigue, joint pressure, knee angle — stream in from our simulator. When the AI spots compensation, it warns right away. The doctor sees a one-week AI digest, adjusts the training plan, and sends it back in one click. One loop. Ninety seconds. Live.
>
> Take the AI away, and it collapses: the check-in becomes a form, the charts become numbers, the doctor sees nothing. The AI does the understanding, the summary, and the real-time coaching. That is the product.
>
> Competition? Physitrack sends exercise plans — it knows if you did it, not if you did it right. Sword Health proves AI rehab is valuable, but it is a heavy US model: employer-paid, a human care team behind every member. We are light — one doctor plus AI, for Asian families. KinDex proved compensation sensing in a lab. We turned it into a system you can run today.
>
> Who pays? Clinics first, per seat, to follow patients at home. Then patients themselves. Market: MSK digital health reaches **11.6 billion USD by 2030, growing 17.7% a year** — the knee is its biggest, fastest segment.
>
> One compliance line: this is training companion software, **not a medical device**. Judgment stays with professionals. All data is synthetic.
>
> We are five people, design plus engineering. We built the hardware prototype before, and this whole platform during the hackathon. We are looking for clinical advisors and one or two pilot clinics. Zhiyuan has waited six years. Let's make his recovery show up — every day. Thank you.

## 三、答辩弹药库：评委QA Top12（45-60秒英文口头回答 + 一行中文提示）

> 每题按"直接答→给框架→落到演示→收口"展开。语速放慢，宁可短不可赶。

### Q1. Soft Healthcare边界（30秒压缩版见下）

**45-60秒版：**
> Our main demo is a pure software loop — daily check-ins, training companionship, readable summaries. That is exactly what this track calls Soft Healthcare: non-clinical support. We draw three lines, ourselves. First, output: the AI only produces training suggestions and plan drafts — never clinical conclusions. "Risk level" here means a training safety hint: continue, slow down, or pause and see a doctor. Second, decision power: the doctor confirms and sends every plan. The AI never sends anything by itself. Third, presentation: no medical claim words anywhere, a clear disclaimer on every page, and red-flag signals automatically advise seeing a doctor. The hardware is a background vision — not part of this demo. If we ever commercialize for medical use, we go through proper regulatory assessment first.

**一行中文提示：** 三道线自己先划：AI只出训练建议／医生是唯一决策人／全站免责+红灯转介；硬件不在Demo内。

**30秒压缩版：**
> It's training companion software, not a medical device. The AI only suggests training; the doctor decides and sends the plan. Red flags advise seeing a doctor, disclaimers are on every page, and the hardware is not in this demo.

### Q2. 把AI拿掉会怎样（30秒压缩版见下）

**45-60秒版：**
> You get a questionnaire plus a chart library. Look at what the AI actually does, layer by layer. Understanding: it turns free-form check-ins into a personal training suggestion for that day — a fixed form cannot do that. Summary: the doctor faces a full week of multi-channel data; the AI compresses it into trend, pattern, and risk. Doctors read the digest, not the raw curves — that is process redesign, not text generation. Interaction: compensation warnings during training are driven by the live data stream, not pre-written lines. And every loop cycle adds personal history, so suggestions get sharper over time. We use DeepSeek — structured summarization is its sweet spot, and it only reads structured check-in data, which keeps hallucination low. Remove the AI, and the loop collapses. That is the test this track asks for — we welcome it.

**一行中文提示：** 去掉AI=问卷+图表库；理解/摘要/交互三层各指一屏；点名DeepSeek+只读结构化数据=幻觉可控。

**30秒压缩版：**
> A questionnaire plus a chart library. The AI does three real jobs: personal daily suggestions from check-ins, a one-week digest doctors actually read, and live compensation warnings. Remove it — the loop collapses.

### Q3. 合成数据可信度（30秒压缩版见下）

**45-60秒版：**
> Fair question. Synthetic data proves one thing: the product logic and the workflow work. It does not prove clinical outcomes — we keep those two separate. Three points. First, compliance: this track only allows synthetic or public anonymized data, and we fully comply — it is stated in the footer. Second, control: we can reproduce compensation, over-limit, and red-flag referral scenes safely and repeatably — you just saw them on demand; real patient data could never be demoed like this. Third, the simulator's interface is designed as the future hardware interface — the safety state machine is built for real data, not faked for the stage. Our validation path: public anonymized datasets, then small wearable tests, then pilot users. And honestly — zero real users today. That is exactly why we are here, looking for our first pilots.

**一行中文提示：** 合成数据只证"逻辑成立"不证效果；能安全复现红灯场景是优点；验证三步走；大方承认0用户。

**30秒压缩版：**
> Synthetic data proves the logic works — not clinical outcomes; we separate the two. It lets us replay risky scenes safely, and the simulator interface is the future hardware interface. Next: public datasets, small wearable tests, then pilots.

### Q4. 谁付钱（30秒压缩版见下）

**45-60秒版：**
> Today, nobody — this stage proves the loop, and we are honest about that. Near term, the easiest payer is the clinic or hospital: they need to follow discharged patients at home, and paying per clinician seat for a home-exercise tool is already a proven model — Physitrack runs on it. Mid term, patients themselves: recovery is long and high-frequency, and paying means no repeated hospital trips — Hong Kong's private market fits this. Long term, employers and insurers: MSK is a top driver of workplace cost, and outcome-based payment exists — but we only walk that road after real outcome data. This industry's lesson is clear: companies with only a reimbursement story die. So we start where money already flows — clinic tools, Hong Kong first, then the Greater Bay Area.

**一行中文提示：** 近期诊所按席位付费（Physitrack已验证）；中期患者自费；远期雇主保险需真实效果数据；不靠报销讲故事。

**30秒压缩版：**
> Clinics first — per-seat payment to follow patients at home, a proven model. Then patients self-pay to skip repeated hospital trips. Employers and insurers later, only after real outcome data. No reimbursement fantasy.

### Q5. 与Physitrack/Hinge等竞品的差异

**45-60秒版：**
> We won't pretend a five-person team beats a hundred-thousand-clinician platform at its own game — exercise libraries are not our battlefield. Three differences. Input: they know whether the patient did the exercises; we add whether they did them right — fatigue and compensation signals from our data stream. Output: their doctor gets a checklist; our doctor gets an AI digest with one-click plan adjustment. Stage: they are mature companies; we are a prototype testing one assumption — quality data plus an AI loop, at home. On Hinge and Sword: their IPO and valuations actually help us — they prove payers fund MSK. We take the slot they don't cover: a lightweight, doctor-patient closed loop for Asian families. One line: we don't build a bigger exercise library — we make home-training quality visible, and close the loop.

**一行中文提示：** 不比动作库；输入差（对不对）、产出差（AI摘要vs清单）、阶段差（假设验证）；Hinge/Sword替我们教育了市场。

### Q6. 医生真的会用吗

**45-60秒版：**
> Honest answer: we have not done formal physician interviews yet — that is our next step. But the design has one rule: give the doctor nothing new to do. Physician burnout comes from screen burden. So our doctor side has one goal — understand a week in thirty seconds. The AI digest replaces raw curves; adjusting a plan is one click. We don't add a screen — we read one screen for the doctor. And I'd like to flip this question into a demo: sir, would you play the doctor for ninety seconds and walk the loop yourself? Our validation checklist: baseline follow-up time, trust in AI digests, tolerance for false alarms, and willingness to pay. We will prove it with data — not adjectives.

**一行中文提示：** 承认未做正式访谈；设计唯一标准=30秒看懂一周；现场邀请评委扮演医生（最强回应）；下一步访谈清单四项。

### Q7. 依从性衰减：第8周还打卡吗

**45-60秒版：**
> You are right — industry data shows digital interventions boost adherence mostly in the first eight to twelve weeks, then it fades. We don't claim to be the exception. We design mechanisms, not promises. One: the daily check-in takes under a minute. Two: the loop makes care visible — patients see the plan change the same day they report. Someone is watching; a pure exercise-library app cannot do that. Three: the doctor's involvement turns adherence into a shared responsibility, not willpower. Four: progress reports and correction cards give immediate positive feedback. And we will measure ourselves harshly: our first pilot metric is weekly retention and check-in streaks — not downloads. If the numbers are bad, we will know early, and iterate.

**一行中文提示：** 先承认行业衰减规律；四机制（低门槛/被看见/医患共担/即时正反馈）；第一指标=周留存，不是下载量。

### Q8. 硬件护膝做得出来吗

**45-60秒版：**
> It is not a PowerPoint vision. Before this hackathon, we built a working Arduino prototype — real sEMG electrodes, air pump, relay. The photos are on this table, with standing-versus-bending waveform charts. What we verified: the sense-and-act loop, and the safety state machine — over-limit means stop inflation, deflate immediately, prompt a pause. What we have not verified — honestly: long-wear reliability, sensor calibration, unit cost, biocompatibility. That is our milestone list, not our claim list. And notice the software mirror: the simulator in this demo runs the same safety logic. Software first carries the data structure; hardware follows — monitoring before support. If it ever claims medical use, it goes through regulatory assessment — which is exactly why it stays a background vision today.

**一行中文提示：** 桌上实拍=不是纸面愿景；已验证回路+安全状态机；未验证四项如实列出；软硬同构，监测先行。

### Q9. 健康数据隐私与跨境

**45-60秒版：**
> Today's facts first: all data in this demo is synthetic — zero real, identifiable health data, stated in the footer. For the real architecture, four principles. Collect the minimum. De-identify before anything reaches the AI layer or any LLM call. Doctors only see patients they are bound to. And deployment region plus cross-border compliance — Hong Kong's PDPO, the mainland's PIPL — are front-loaded assessments before commercialization, not patches after. I won't recite legal clauses here; the honest answer is: the demo carries zero real data, and formal compliance assessment comes before any real deployment. Privacy is a design constraint for us — not a checkbox.

**一行中文提示：** 演示零真实数据；四原则（最小化/先脱敏再进AI/授权访问/跨境前置评估）；不背法条，姿态收口。

### Q10. 团队凭什么做医疗

**45-60秒版：**
> Five of us — industrial design plus engineering. Fair concern, so let the work speak. Before the competition, we independently built the hardware prototype and ran the user research — that is on this table. During the hackathon, we built the entire three-sided web platform and the AI layer from zero — the git log proves the timeline. This demo is our capability test. Our clinical knowledge is the gap, and we say it plainly: that is why our first ask is a rehabilitation advisor and pilot partners. We know exactly what we are missing and what we need. We think that is a strength, not a weakness.

**一行中文提示：** 让作品说话（赛前硬件+赛中三端，git log为证）；短板=临床，所以今天的诉求就是顾问与试点。

### Q11. 为什么现在、为什么膝盖（Lan Lu·长寿视角）

**45-60秒版：**
> Knees, because knee problems travel with aging — post-surgery, degeneration, sports injury. And now, three forces line up. Hospitals discharge patients earlier, so the home gap grows. Rehab professionals are scarce, so software is the cheapest way into the living room. And LLMs just made personal companionship and doctor-side summaries nearly free, while sensing costs keep falling — quality data at home is newly possible. For the longevity lens: an older adult training safely at home — seen, supported, corrected — is one concrete step of healthspan. Knees are the first joint; the same loop extends to more joints and daily chronic-condition support. Software first, then our smart brace. That is our longevity-tech path for Asia-Pacific.

**一行中文提示：** 早出院→居家缺口扩大；康复人力稀缺；LLM+传感成本下降=为什么是现在；膝是长寿科技的第一站，软硬两条曲线。

### Q12. 比赛之后：第一批用户从哪来

**45-60秒版：**
> Three steps — no five-year curves. Step one, validation: small-scale wearable tests plus physician interviews; we replace synthetic scenes with real data structures. Step two, seeds: one or two rehab departments or clinics as pilots, on the per-seat tool model. Step three, decide with data: retention and digest quality tell us whether to go deeper as a clinic tool, or toward patient self-pay. And our concrete ask today: a clinical advisor, introductions to pilot departments, and Cyberport ecosystem support. We are not asking for belief — we are asking for the first two doctors who will test it with us.

**一行中文提示：** 三步（验证→种子→数据定主航道）；具体点名要什么：临床顾问+试点引荐+数码港对接；不画营收曲线。
